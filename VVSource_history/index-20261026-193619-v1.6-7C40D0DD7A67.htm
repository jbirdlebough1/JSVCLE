Vinyl Cutter Simulator

Choose the color of vinyl you'll be cutting. Your drawing runs through a virtual plotter, then the unwanted vinyl is weeded (removed) so only your design is left on the backing sheet.
Before you start: a vinyl cutter only cuts — it cannot engrave or rasterize. Only objects with a Hairline outline are cut. Fills, thicker outlines, images and any other objects are ignored.
